Unmodified upstream OTF. Application bitmap subsets are generated at
12, 14, 18 and 44 pixels. The 44px decimal digits are losslessly run-length
encoded for the application font region; they are not a new upstream font.

The existing Black font remains used for other application sizes.
Exact hashes of both source files are listed in ../preview-fonts.json.
No font binaries were extracted from the third-party 4.2-inch firmware.
